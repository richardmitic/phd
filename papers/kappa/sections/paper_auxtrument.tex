\subsubsection*{Research methods}
This paper presents a retrospective of a project to build a distributed audio performance sending stereo audio over mobile networks using the WebRTC standard protocol. It follows the practice-based research methodology as defined by \citeauthorandnumber{candyPracticeBasedResearch2006}.

\subsubsection*{Results and conclusions}
The research journey documented in the paper serves as both a map and proverbial \q{early warning system} for future researchers or artists who are undertaking similar audio projects. It highlights that while focus might initially be on the sonic material, practical and technical issues have the potential to become disproportionately engaging. One particular conclusion was that network reliability was a limiting factor of the audio performance, sometimes leading to a degraded spatial impression in the transmitted audio. This addressed \rqa{} in proving that the technological context surrounding the audio signal itself can make an equally large impression on the stereo field as the musical content.
